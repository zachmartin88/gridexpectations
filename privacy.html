Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

Short version: Grid Expectations doesn't collect any data. There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and we have no servers. Your PDFs and progress stay on your device and, if you use iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud.

Who we are

Grid Expectations is made by ZMRTN LLC ("we"). This policy covers the Grid Expectations app for iPhone and iPad and this website.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-up, no analytics or crash-reporting tools, no advertising, and no third-party tracking code. It doesn't send your PDFs, your stitches, or anything about how you use the app to us or to anyone else. We can't see any of it.

What stays on your device

When you import a chart, the app copies the PDF into its own private storage on your device. Your stitch progress, parking pins, snapshots, your answers from the confirm step, your stats and your settings are stored there too. Deleting a chart removes the app's copy and its progress. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device.

Your private iCloud

If iCloud sync is on, the app stores the same files in your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit, so your charts and progress appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. That database belongs to your Apple Account: only you can read it, and we can't. It counts toward your iCloud storage and is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

You can turn sync off at any time in the app under Settings › Sync with iCloud. Data already in iCloud can be removed in the iOS Settings app under your Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage. Deleting a chart with sync on also deletes it from your iCloud. The built-in sample chart never syncs.

Purchases

Grid Expectations Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and your payment details; we never see them. The app uses Apple's StoreKit on your device only to check whether Pro is active, and it keeps that answer on your device so the app works offline.

Your PDFs are your patterns

We never upload, share, sell or change your PDFs. The app reads them on your device to build the chart you mark.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub, as the host, may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your data is under your control on your device and in your iCloud account, as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting data, we'll say so here and in the App Store listing before it does.

Contact

Questions about privacy? See the support page for how to reach us.
