Grid Expectations turns the cross-stitch PDF charts you own into charts you can mark, stitch by stitch, on iPad and iPhone. Here are answers to the most common questions.

Importing charts

How do I add a chart?

Tap + (Add chart) in the library and pick one or more PDFs from Files. You can also open a PDF in Mail, Safari or Files and tap Share › Grid Expectations. Shop downloads usually land in Files › Downloads.

What happens to my PDF?

The app keeps its own copy and reads it on your device. It finds the grid and the key, and counts every stitch. Your original file stays where it was.

What do the labels on a chart mean?

  • Stitches verified (green): every color's count matched the counts printed in the key.
  • All matched: every symbol was matched to the key, but the key has no counts to check against.
  • Confirm N symbols or Name 1 thread: a quick confirm step is needed (see below).
  • Choose from N designs: the PDF holds more than one design; choose the one you're stitching.
  • This chart is a picture: the chart is a scanned or flattened image rather than a drawn chart, so the app can't read it reliably. It tells you instead of guessing.

Tap Import details in a chart's menu to see the reasons in plain words. Backstitch, fractional stitches, French knots and beads aren't shown in this version; the details list them if your chart has them.

Can I try it without a chart?

Yes. Tap Try the sample chart in the empty library, or Settings › Add the sample chart. It's a small chart we made, so you can practice marking.

The confirm step

Why does the app ask me to confirm symbols?

Some PDFs draw a symbol slightly differently in the chart than in the key. Instead of guessing, the app shows you each unclear symbol, cropped from your own PDF, next to its stitch count, a mini-map of where it appears, and its best suggestion.

How do I answer?

Open the step from the chart's banner, the import details, or the chart's menu in the library. For each symbol, tap Yes, that's it, Something else (pick a key row, a DMC thread, or type any brand), or Not a stitch for markers and notes. Accept all suggestions answers everything in one tap. Nothing changes until you tap, and you can see your key at any time with See your key.

How do I know my answers are right?

After every answer the app re-checks the chart. "Every count matches the key — verified" means the printed counts agree with your answers. If some counts don't match, it tells you which to look at again. When a key has no counts, the result says "confirmed by you" instead of "verified".

Will confirming lose my progress?

No. Answers never change the grid, so your stitches stay put. Only switching to a different design in a multi-design PDF starts that chart fresh, and the app asks first.

iCloud sync

How does sync work?

With iCloud sync on, your charts, stitches, snapshots, confirmations and stats sync through your own private iCloud database to your other iPhones and iPads signed in to the same Apple Account. Turn it on or off in Settings › Sync with iCloud.

What if I stitch on two devices while offline?

Everything is saved on the device first. When the devices reconnect, every stitch from both is kept. If you un-marked a stitch on one device, the newest change wins for that stitch.

The library says "On this device" or "iCloud paused"

"On this device" means sync is off or the device isn't signed in to iCloud. "Will sync when online" means you're offline and nothing is lost. "iCloud paused" means iCloud couldn't take the latest changes, most often because your iCloud storage is full. Settings in the app shows the reason, and your changes stay safe on the device until it clears.

I imported the same PDF on two devices

The app recognizes the same PDF and keeps one chart, combining the progress from both.

Purchases

What's free and what's Pro?

The free plan tracks one active chart at a time with every feature. Viewing, imports and confirm checks are unlimited. Pro tracks every chart you have on the go, as a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Progress is never locked, even if Pro ends.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases (it's also on the Pro screen). Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you bought Pro with. A lifetime purchase can be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

In the app, Settings › Manage subscription, or in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
