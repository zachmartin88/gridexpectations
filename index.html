Great expectations,
one stitch at a time.

Grid Expectations turns the cross-stitch PDF charts you already own into charts you can mark as you stitch, on iPad and iPhone.

Coming soon to the App Store
1

Bring your PDF

Import from Files, Mail or Safari. The app reads the grid and the key, and counts every stitch.

2

Check it in a tap

If a symbol is unclear, a quick confirm step shows it next to your key. The printed counts double-check the answer.

3

Mark as you go

Separate Move and Mark modes, 10×10 blocks, parking pins and Apple Pencil, with undo for every step.

4

Never lose a stitch

Autosave, daily snapshots and sync through your own iCloud. Two devices offline? Every stitch from both is kept.

Your charts stay yours. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your PDFs and progress live on your device and in your own private iCloud, never on a server of ours. Read the privacy policy.
