Your cross-stitch charts, marked stitch by stitch. And never lost.

Grid Expectations turns the PDF charts you already own into charts you can mark as you stitch, on iPad and iPhone. No highlighter, no sticky notes, no wondering where you were.

Free for one chart. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Your progress is safe

Every stitch is saved a moment after you mark it. A snapshot is kept from the start of each day you stitch, undo works even after you close the app, and your own iCloud keeps your iPad and iPhone in step, offline included. If your device is ever full, the app says so and keeps trying.

Bring the PDF you have

Open a chart from Files, Mail or Safari. The app reads the grid and the key on your device and counts every stitch. When every count matches your key, the chart is marked verified. An unclear symbol? It shows you the symbol as printed, with a suggestion, and one tap confirms it.

Made for stitching

Separate Move and Mark modes, so a finger never marks by accident. Fill a 10×10 block, park your threads, highlight one color and hop to its next stitch. Apple Pencil marks while your fingers pan. Big buttons, large text and a true dark mode.

Roomy on iPad, with every color beside the chart. Handy on iPhone when you're stitching on the go.
